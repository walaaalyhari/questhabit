## 🎭 The Core Mechanics

### 1. Health (❤️)
Your health represents your consistency. You start with a full health bar.
* **Taking Damage:** If you fail to complete a **Daily Quest** or a **Scheduled Quest** on its required day, your health will decrease.
* **Healing:** Completing quests consistently will slowly regenerate your health.
* **Game Over:** If your health drops to zero, your level, XP, and streak will be reset. Be careful!

### 2. Experience & Leveling (✨)
Completing quests grants you Experience Points (XP).
* **Leveling Up:** Once the XP bar fills up, your character will Level Up!
* **Difficulty Multiplier:** Harder quests (Epic, Legendary) yield significantly more XP than Common ones.

### 3. Streaks (🔥)
Doing the same quest consecutive days in a row builds a Streak.
* Streaks apply a multiplier to your XP gains, making it the fastest way to level up.
* Missing a day breaks the streak.

### 4. Hydration (💧)
Staying hydrated is critical for any adventurer!
* Create quests with the **Fluid** type.
* Completing these will fill your Hydration meter.
* Reaching your daily hydration goal will reward you with rare **Diamonds**.

## 📜 Quest Types & Difficulties

When adding a new quest by tapping the **+** button, you can customize it:

### Types
* **Daily:** A quest that resets every single day. Great for building rock-solid habits.
* **Scheduled:** A quest that only appears on specific days of the week (e.g., "Gym on Monday, Wednesday, Friday").
* **One-Time:** A specific task you only need to do once (e.g., "Call the doctor"). Once completed, it moves to your Archive.
* **Fluid:** A special water-tracking quest to fill your hydration meter.

### Difficulties
* **Common (Grey):** Easy, quick tasks (e.g., "Make bed"). Yields standard XP and a few Coins.
* **Rare (Blue):** Takes some effort (e.g., "Read 10 pages"). Yields more XP and Coins.
* **Epic (Purple):** Hard tasks (e.g., "1 hour workout"). High XP and Coin rewards.
* **Legendary (Gold):** Monumental tasks. Massive XP and a chance for Diamonds!

## 🛍️ The Rewards System

What's an RPG without loot?

* **Coins (🪙):** Earned from almost every quest.
* **Diamonds (💎):** Rare currency earned from Hydration goals and Legendary quests.

Navigate to the **Rewards** tab to spend your hard-earned currency. You can treat yourself to real-life rewards you define, like "Eat a cheat meal", "Play 2 hours of video games", or "Buy a new book".

---

## ⚙️ Settings & Management

Tap the **Settings (Three Dashes)** in your dashboard header to:
* **Customize Avatar:** Change your player name and emoji avatar.
* **Toggle Dark Mode:** Switch between the Day (Light) and Night (Dark) sky backgrounds.
* **Archive:** View past completed quests.
* **Reset Data:** If you want a fresh start, you can permanently erase all your progress and habits here.

Good luck on your adventure!
